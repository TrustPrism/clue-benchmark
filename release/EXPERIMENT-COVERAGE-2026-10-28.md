# Results-page experiment coverage — October 28, 2026

The public results page now covers eight study families with equal navigation cards and equally ranked sections. Each comparison names the question, reports observed outcomes, explains its interpretation and states its limits.

## Coverage

1. Original low-thinking pilot: delivery and missing evidence; irrelevant-record doses; false-report counts; identical-payload response variation.
2. High-thinking controls: path/tree structure; random/targeted corruption; connected truthful additions; finite output resources.
3. Flash development stress tests: full size ladder; matched output-budget increase; unavoidable-violation construction; two-world repeated confirmation.
4. Fresh hard-set confirmation: all 48 fresh cases, errors, abstentions, uncertainty and resources.
5. Initial development screening: all Flash and original Qwen conditions, interruptions and unattempted slots.
6. Qwen settings diagnostic: all nine outcomes across three cases/configurations, including reuse in the later panel.
7. Full sampled-Qwen panel: all nine conditions and the matched 29-slot completion/correctness comparison.
8. Nuisance replication: initial and extension phases, both necessary-evidence strata, combined uncertainty and settled costs.

Offline solver, construction-heuristic and invariance checks are separately labeled. Prepared but unexecuted arms are not presented as measured results. Shared worlds, repeated attempts and reused receipts are not summed into an independent-case total.
