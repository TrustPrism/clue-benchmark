Open questions for the initial preview

This is an early research preview of Clue Benchmark 0.5.0rc2. The measured results are preliminary; the software candidate, redistribution licenses and human reviews remain pending. The owner requested an initial public preview with those questions visible. This does not grant an open-source or dataset license or claim a PyPI release.

Questions the experiments leave open

  1. Does irrelevant evidence ever help consistently? The original five-world Lite improvement did not repeat in the expanded follow-up. The primary endpoint change was zero, with a descriptive interval of [-25, +25] percentage points on six worlds. A larger, separately preregistered sample would be needed to distinguish a repeatable benefit from sampling and response variation.
  2. Why did individual worlds improve or worsen? Four primary worlds stayed unchanged, one improved and one worsened. These outcomes do not identify a reasoning mechanism. Content, order and context length need separate controls before attributing changes to distraction or useful extra computation.
  3. Is the harder-setting dip repeatable? Lite's 9/12 → 5/12 → 7/12 pattern at 0/100/1,000 irrelevant records is small and non-monotonic. Does it recur on fresh worlds, and how much is ordinary response variation?
  4. When does robustness become worth its cost? In the four-report setting, cost rose about 56× for Lite and 77× for Flash at 1,000 records without higher endpoint accuracy. Does that tradeoff change with useful redundancy, related distractors, other models or a different resource allowance?
  5. What makes a certified puzzle difficult for an agent? Minimum necessary evidence measures information burden, not proof depth or model difficulty. Which structural properties predict errors after controlling for simple construction heuristics, input length and output budget?
  6. How stable are results across resource settings and model versions? The adaptive six-world budget comparison and fresh 48-world hard confirmation answer different questions. Fresh matched comparisons are needed before generalizing across thinking/output allowances, formats or model revisions.
  7. Can agents learn to communicate the right evidence? Current star forwarding is programmed. It demonstrates delivery sufficiency, not learned collaboration or efficient communication policies.
  8. Do supported correctness and completion improve together? Qwen's matched returned slots showed more completed actions without higher supported correctness. Future work should retain lucky guesses, appropriate abstentions, wrong answers and protocol failures as separate outcomes.
  9. Do independent people interpret the rules as intended? Two independent template/rule reviews and an outside installation/replay run remain pending. Automated solver checks do not substitute for these reviews.

These are proposed research questions, not registered experiments or authorized spending. The October 27 allowance is closed at an estimated $6.96, with no holds and no further paid calls. Any future study requires a separate plan and budget.

Questions before a full software and data release

  • Rights and licenses: Does TrustPrism have redistribution rights for all imported code, contributor work, generated assets and saved provider outcomes? Are Apache-2.0 for code and CC BY 4.0 for data/prose the final approved scopes?
  • Attribution and maintenance: Who are the confirmed maintainers and citation authors, and which notices or exclusions are required?
  • Human validation: Who will complete the two rule reviews and outside installation/reproduction review, and how will disagreements be recorded?
  • Historical archive boundary: Which additional reproduction archives may be distributed after manual privacy review? The owner authorized public visibility of the existing repository and its history. The documentation deployment excludes credentials, authorizations, ledgers and raw provider prose; ignored operational archives remain local and are not uploaded.
  • Distribution: Which organization-owned PyPI and dataset accounts will host approved artifacts? Are protected environments, trusted publishing and final hosted dataset/Croissant checks configured?
  • Final acceptance: Which exact source revision and artifact hashes will the owner approve for the complete release? Public preview availability does not clear these gates.
