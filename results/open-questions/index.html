RESEARCH PREVIEW 0.5.0rc2

What is still unanswered?¶

The preview is public. The research findings are preliminary, and the full licensed release still has unfinished reviews.

Research questions¶

Does irrelevant evidence ever help consistently?¶

The original five-world Lite improvement did not repeat in the expanded follow-up. The primary change was zero, with a descriptive interval of −25 to +25 percentage points on six worlds.

What would help: a larger, separately preregistered fresh sample that distinguishes a repeatable benefit from sampling and response variation.

Why did individual worlds change?¶

Four primary worlds stayed unchanged, one improved and one worsened. Those outcomes do not identify a reasoning mechanism.

What would help: separate controls for content, report order and context length before attributing the changes to distraction or useful extra computation.

Is the harder-setting dip repeatable?¶

Lite's 9/12 → 5/12 → 7/12 pattern is small and non-monotonic.

What would help: fresh matched worlds and a better estimate of ordinary response variation.

When is extra context worth the cost?¶

In the four-report setting, costs rose roughly 56× for Lite and 77× for Flash without higher endpoint accuracy.

What would help: comparisons involving useful redundancy, related distractors, different models and explicit resource allowances.

What makes a puzzle difficult?¶

Minimum necessary evidence measures information burden, not proof depth or model difficulty.

What would help: structural predictors of errors that control for construction heuristics, input length and output budget.

How stable are results across model and budget changes?¶

The adaptive six-world output-budget comparison and fresh 48-world confirmation answer different questions.

What would help: fresh matched comparisons before generalizing across thinking budgets, output allowances, input formats or model revisions.

Can agents learn useful communication?¶

Current star forwarding is programmed. It demonstrates evidence delivery, not learned collaboration or an efficient communication policy.

What would help: an explicitly different experiment measuring what agents choose to send and whether those choices deliver sufficient evidence.

Do completion and supported correctness improve together?¶

Qwen's matched returned slots showed more completed actions without higher supported correctness.

What would help: reporting lucky guesses, appropriate abstentions, wrong answers and protocol failures separately in future studies.

Do independent readers agree with the rules?¶

Two independent template/rule reviews and an outside installation/replay run remain pending. Automated solver checks do not replace those reviews.

These are questions, not funded experiments

The October 27 allowance is closed at an estimated $6.96, with no holds. No further paid calls are planned. Any future study needs a separate plan and budget.
